## 01 · The Approach｜新方法

**题材** 学术 / 认知　　**收录** 12 词：stick · stimulate · stir · stipulate · infer · inference · inferior · influence · influential · inform · information · approach

### 英文故事

When Lin Mei's first paper was rejected, the reviewer's comment was brief: the conclusion was interesting, but the evidence did not **stick**. The words stung, yet they also **stimulated** her to reopen the experiment she had closed months before. She refused to let one failure **stir** self-doubt — instead, she wanted to stir a real debate.

The journal **stipulated** that every claim rest on published data, so Lin spent weeks cleaning her tables. From the noise she began to **infer** a pattern, and her **inference**, though bold, was hard to ignore once the **information** was free for anyone to check. Even an **influential** critic wrote back, admitting the new **approach** deserved a second look.

"Never let an **inferior** instrument hide a good question," the critic told her. Under his **influence**, Lin rebuilt the model, and her follow-up paper — the one the same journal now rushed into print — was proof that honest criticism can **inform** better science.

### 考点卡片

#### 01 · stick　`/stɪk/`　*n. / v.*

> 故事义 站得住脚　·　常考义 戳；插入；粘贴；棍，棒

- **核心搭配**　`stick to sth` 坚持（做）某事　·　`stick out` 伸出；显眼　·　`stick with` 继续做
- **近义替换**　adhere · cling · attach
- **词族延伸**　sticky adj. 黏的 → stuck（stick - stuck - stuck） n. 相关延伸
- **易混辨析**　stick to doing 是「坚持做」；insist on doing 是「坚决主张」，搭配不同
- **加分句式**　Good habits stick only when they are easy to begin. 好习惯只有在容易开始时才能坚持下来。

#### 02 · stimulate　`/ˈstɪmjuleɪt/`　*vt.*

> 故事义 激发　·　常考义 刺激；使兴奋

- **核心搭配**　`stimulate demand / growth` 刺激需求、增长　·　`stimulate sb to do sth` 刺激/激励某人做某事　·　`stimulate interest in` 激发对…的兴趣
- **近义替换**　spur · motivate · provoke
- **词族延伸**　stimulus n. 刺激物（复数 stimuli） → stimulation n. 激励
- **易混辨析**　stimulate 是「激发」；simulate 是「模拟」，形近词义完全不同
- **加分句式**　The government introduced tax cuts to stimulate the economy. 政府推出减税措施以刺激经济。

#### 03 · stir　`/stɜː(r)/`　*v. / n.*

> 故事义 激起　·　常考义 引起；激起；激动

- **核心搭配**　`stir up` 挑起，激起　·　`cause a stir` 引起轰动　·　`stir sb to action` 激励某人行动
- **近义替换**　provoke · arouse · agitate
- **词族延伸**　stirred / stirring（stir - stirred - stirred） n. 相关延伸
- **易混辨析**　stir up 多指「挑起情绪或麻烦」；stimulate 侧重「激发积极性、需求」
- **加分句式**　The old photograph stirred memories of a forgotten childhood. 那张旧照片唤起了对被遗忘童年的记忆。

#### 04 · stipulate　`/ˈstɪpjuleɪt/`　*v.*

> 故事义 规定　·　常考义 规定；明确要求

- **核心搭配**　`stipulate that...` 规定…　·　`stipulate sth as a condition` 以…为条件　·　`stipulate for sth` 对…作出规定
- **近义替换**　specify · prescribe · require
- **词族延伸**　stipulation n. 条款，规定
- **易混辨析**　stipulate 指「（合同、规则）明文规定」；regulate 指「依规章管制」
- **加分句式**　The contract stipulates that payment must be made in advance. 合同规定款项必须提前支付。

#### 05 · infer　`/ɪnˈfɜː(r)/`　*vt.*

> 故事义 推断　·　常考义 推断

- **核心搭配**　`infer sth from sth` 从…推断出　·　`infer from the evidence` 从证据中推断　·　`be inferred from` 从…推断出
- **近义替换**　deduce · conclude · surmise
- **词族延伸**　inference n. 推断 → inferable adj. 可推知的
- **易混辨析**　infer 是听者「从证据推出结论」；imply 是说者「话语中暗示」，方向相反
- **加分句式**　We can infer from the data that demand is recovering. 我们可以从数据中推断出需求正在复苏。

#### 06 · inference　`/ˈɪnfərəns/`　*n.*

> 故事义 推断结论　·　常考义 推论；推理；推断结果

- **核心搭配**　`draw an inference from` 从…得出结论　·　`by inference` 据推断　·　`a reasonable inference` 合理的推论
- **近义替换**　conclusion · deduction · reasoning
- **词族延伸**　infer v. → inference n.
- **易混辨析**　inference 是「推出来的结论」；reference 是「参考；提及」，形近常混
- **加分句式**　Such an inference would be premature without more evidence. 没有更多证据就下这样的结论还为时过早。

#### 07 · inferior　`/ɪnˈfɪəriə(r)/`　*adj. / n.*

> 故事义 更差的　·　常考义 下等的；下级的；差的；次的

- **核心搭配**　`inferior to` 比…差（不可用 than）　·　`inferior goods` 劣质品　·　`feel inferior` 感到自卑
- **近义替换**　secondary · substandard · mediocre
- **词族延伸**　inferiority n. 低劣；自卑感
- **易混辨析**　inferior to / superior to 中的 to 都是介词，不能用 more inferior 或 than
- **加分句式**　No language is inferior to another; each carries its own worldview. 没有哪种语言比另一种低等；每种语言都承载着自己的世界观。

#### 08 · influence　`/ˈɪnfluəns/`　*n. / v.*

> 故事义 影响　·　常考义 影响

- **核心搭配**　`have an influence on` 对…有影响　·　`under the influence of` 在…影响下　·　`influence sb against sth` （相关常用表达）
- **近义替换**　affect · sway · impact
- **词族延伸**　influential adj. 有影响力的
- **易混辨析**　influence 名动两用；affect 只作动词，effect 多作名词，三者常同框设项
- **加分句式**　Parents have a lasting influence on how children handle failure. 父母对孩子如何面对失败有着持久的影响。

#### 09 · influential　`/ˌɪnfluˈenʃl/`　*adj.*

> 故事义 有影响力的　·　常考义 有影响的；权势的

- **核心搭配**　`an influential figure` 有影响力的人物　·　`be influential in doing sth` （相关常用表达）　·　`a highly influential essay` （相关常用表达）
- **近义替换**　powerful · prominent · authoritative
- **词族延伸**　influence → influential → influentially adv.
- **易混辨析**　influential 修饰「人、观点、机构」；effective 修饰「方法、措施」，表「有效的」
- **加分句式**　Her influential essay changed how schools teach history. 她那篇有影响力的文章改变了学校教授历史的方式。

#### 10 · inform　`/ɪnˈfɔːm/`　*v.*

> 故事义 告知　·　常考义 通知；告诉

- **核心搭配**　`inform sb of / about sth` 通知某人某事　·　`keep sb informed` 让某人知情　·　`inform against sb` 告发某人
- **近义替换**　notify · tell · apprise
- **词族延伸**　information n. 信息 → informed adj. 有见识的；misinform v. 误导
- **易混辨析**　inform sb of sth 必须带 of，不能说 inform sb sth，这是高频语法考点
- **加分句式**　Citizens should be fully informed of the risks before signing. 公民在签字前应被充分告知风险。

#### 11 · information　`/ˌɪnfəˈmeɪʃn/`　*n.*

> 故事义 信息，资料　·　常考义 情报；资料；信息

- **核心搭配**　`gather / collect information` （相关常用表达）　·　`a piece of information` 一条信息　·　`information technology` 信息技术
- **近义替换**　data · intelligence · material
- **词族延伸**　inform v. → information n. → informative adj. 信息量大的
- **易混辨析**　information 不可数，「一条信息」用 a piece of information，考试高频
- **加分句式**　The Internet gives us instant access to vast amounts of information. 互联网让我们即时获取海量信息。

#### 12 · approach　`/əˈprəʊtʃ/`　*n. / v.*

> 故事义 方法；新路径　·　常考义 方法；态度；靠近；接近；（着手）处理；（开始）对付

- **核心搭配**　`an approach to (doing) sth ……` 的方法　·　`adopt / take an approach` （相关常用表达）　·　`approach sb about sth` 就…找某人商量
- **近义替换**　method · way · come near
- **词族延伸**　approachable adj. 可亲近的
- **易混辨析**　approach to 的 to 是介词，后接 doing；method 常搭配 of / for
- **加分句式**　We need a fresh approach to dealing with urban waste. 我们需要一种处理城市垃圾的新思路。

### 本故事词表

stick 站得住脚 · stimulate 激发 · stir 激起 · stipulate 规定 · infer 推断 · inference 推断结论 · inferior 更差的 · influence 影响 · influential 有影响力的 · inform 告知 · information 信息，资料 · approach 方法；新路径

## 02 · The Stamp of Approval｜认可的印记

**题材** 法律 / 社会　　**收录** 15 词：appropriate · approval · approve · apt · conform · confront · confuse · confusion · congress · connect · connection · conscious · continual · continue · continuous

### 英文故事

As an aide on the energy committee, Qian was **conscious** of one **continual** complaint: the old grid could not support **continuous** growth. When the **congress** opened its spring session, she drafted a modest proposal to **connect** every village clinic to a stable power source — the **connection** between electricity and survival was, to her, obvious.

Her supervisor warned that any plan failing to **conform** to the budget would never win **approval**. Lobbyists, meanwhile, tried to **confuse** the public, spreading **confusion** about costs until even supporters fell silent. Yet Qian refused to **confront** them with anger; she answered every claim with a spreadsheet.

"The only **appropriate** standard is what villages can afford," she told the committee, and the **apt** phrase carried the vote. The bill passed with broad approval, and the chairman — who rarely **approved** of anything — signed it with a smile. Qian **continued** to watch the meters, certain that policy, like power, must travel the last mile.

### 考点卡片

#### 13 · appropriate　`/əˈprəʊpriət/`　*adj.*

> 故事义 恰当的　·　常考义 恰当的

- **核心搭配**　`be appropriate for / to` 适合…　·　`at the appropriate time` （相关常用表达）　·　`it is appropriate that...` （从句用虚拟）
- **近义替换**　suitable · fitting · proper
- **词族延伸**　appropriately adv.；inappropriate adj. 不恰当的
- **易混辨析**　appropriate 是「恰当得体」；approximate 是「大约的」，形近易混
- **加分句式**　Casual dress is not appropriate for a formal interview. 休闲装不适合正式面试。

#### 14 · approval　`/əˈpruːvl/`　*n.*

> 故事义 批准；赞成　·　常考义 赞成；批准；同意；认可

- **核心搭配**　`win / gain approval` 获得批准　·　`meet with approval` 得到认可　·　`seal of approval` 正式认可
- **近义替换**　consent · endorsement · authorization
- **词族延伸**　approve v. → approval n.；disapproval n. 不赞成
- **易混辨析**　approval 是名词「批准、赞成」；approving 是形容词「赞许的」，词性不同
- **加分句式**　The plan won the approval of the local community. 该计划赢得了当地社区的认可。

#### 15 · approve　`/əˈpruːv/`　*v.*

> 故事义 批准；认可　·　常考义 批准；通过；赞成；同意

- **核心搭配**　`approve sth` 批准某事　·　`approve of (doing) sth` 赞成某事　·　`be approved by` 经…批准
- **近义替换**　endorse · authorize · sanction
- **词族延伸**　approval n.；disapprove v. 不赞成
- **易混辨析**　approve sth 是「官方批准」；approve of sth 是「个人赞同」，有无 of 是考点
- **加分句式**　Parliament approved the bill by a large majority. 议会以多数票批准了该法案。

#### 16 · apt　`/æpt/`　*adj.*

> 故事义 贴切的　·　常考义 易于…的；有…倾向的；恰当的

- **核心搭配**　`be apt to do` 易于做…　·　`an apt description` 贴切的描述　·　`be apt at` 善于
- **近义替换**　likely · prone · fitting
- **词族延伸**　aptly adv. 贴切地；aptitude n. 天资
- **易混辨析**　apt to do 强调「有…倾向（常指负面）」；likely to 只是「有可能」
- **加分句式**　He is apt to forget appointments when deadlines pile up. 截稿期扎堆时，他很容易忘掉约会。

#### 17 · conform　`/kənˈfɔːm/`　*v.*

> 故事义 符合；遵从　·　常考义 一致；符合；遵从

- **核心搭配**　`conform to / with` 符合，遵从　·　`conform to the rules` 遵守规则　·　`conform to a standard` （相关常用表达）
- **近义替换**　comply · abide by · match
- **词族延伸**　conformity n. 遵从；nonconformist n. 不循常规者
- **易混辨析**　conform to 后接「规则、标准」；confirm 是「确认」，形近义异
- **加分句式**　New products must conform to national safety standards. 新产品必须符合国家安

#### 18 · confront　`/kənˈfrʌnt/`　*vt.*

> 故事义 直面　·　常考义 （使）面临；（使）遭遇；勇敢地面对

- **核心搭配**　`be confronted with` 面临　·　`confront the problem` 直面问题　·　`confront sb about sth` 就…与某人对质
- **近义替换**　face · encounter · tackle
- **词族延伸**　confrontation n. 对抗；confrontational adj. 对抗性的
- **易混辨析**　confront 是「主动面对难题」；confuse 是「使糊涂」，同前缀不同根
- **加分句式**　Young graduates are confronted with a tough job market. 年轻毕业生正面临严峻的就业市场。

#### 19 · confuse　`/kənˈfjuːz/`　*vt.*

> 故事义 混淆　·　常考义 使混乱；混淆

- **核心搭配**　`confuse A with / and B` 把 A 与 B 混淆　·　`be confused about` 对…感到困惑　·　`confuse the issue` 搅乱问题
- **近义替换**　mix up · bewilder · muddle
- **词族延伸**　confusion n.；confused adj. 感到困惑的；confusing adj. 令人困惑的
- **易混辨析**　confused 修饰「人感到困惑」；confusing 修饰「事物令人困惑」，常设选项
- **加分句式**　Do not confuse absence of evidence with evidence of absence. 不要把「没有证据」与「证明不存在」混为一谈。

#### 20 · confusion　`/kənˈfjuːʒn/`　*n.*

> 故事义 混乱　·　常考义 混乱；混淆

- **核心搭配**　`cause / lead to confusion` （相关常用表达）　·　`in confusion` 混乱中　·　`confusion between A and B` （相关常用表达）
- **近义替换**　chaos · bewilderment · mix-up
- **词族延伸**　confuse v. → confusion n.
- **易混辨析**　confusion 是「混乱状态」；diffusion 是「扩散」，形近义异
- **加分句式**　There is some confusion between the terms 'skill' and 'talent'. 「技能」和「天赋」这两个概念常被混淆。

#### 21 · congress　`/ˈkɒŋɡres/`　*n.*

> 故事义 （美国）国会　·　常考义 国会；议会；（代表）大会

- **核心搭配**　`the US Congress` 美国国会　·　`a medical congress` 医学大会　·　`Congress passes a bill` 国会通过法案
- **近义替换**　parliament · assembly · convention
- **词族延伸**　congressional adj. 国会的
- **易混辨析**　Congress 大写特指美国国会；conference 只是一般「会议」
- **加分句式**　Congress passed the act after months of debate. 经过数月辩论，国会通过了该法案。

#### 22 · connect　`/kəˈnekt/`　*v.*

> 故事义 连接；联系　·　常考义 连接；联系

- **核心搭配**　`connect A to / with B` 把 A 与 B 相连　·　`be connected with` 与…有关　·　`connect sb with` 为某人接通
- **近义替换**　link · join · associate
- **词族延伸**　connection n.；connected adj.；disconnect v.
- **易混辨析**　connect to 偏「物理连接」；associate with 偏「抽象联想、关联」
- **加分句式**　The study connects sleep quality with academic performance. 该研究把睡眠质量与学业表现联系起来。

#### 23 · connection　`/kəˈnekʃn/`　*n.*

> 故事义 联系　·　常考义 联系；连接

- **核心搭配**　`have a connection with` 与…有联系　·　`in connection with` 关于，与…有关　·　`a network connection` 网络连接
- **近义替换**　link · relation · association
- **词族延伸**　connect v. → connection n.
- **易混辨析**　connection 泛指「联系、连接」；relation 强调「人际或因果关系」
- **加分句式**　There is a clear connection between income and health. 收入与健康之间存在着明显的关联。

#### 24 · conscious　`/ˈkɒnʃəs/`　*adj.*

> 故事义 意识到的　·　常考义 有意识的；意识到的；自觉的

- **核心搭配**　`be conscious of` 意识到　·　`a conscious decision` 慎重的决定　·　`become conscious` 恢复知觉
- **近义替换**　aware · deliberate · mindful
- **词族延伸**　consciousness n. 意识；subconscious adj. 潜意识的；conscience n. 良心
- **易混辨析**　conscious 是「有意识的」；conscience 是「良心」，一词尾之差，高频混淆
- **加分句式**　We are all conscious of the need to save energy. 我们都意识到了节能的必要。

#### 25 · continual　`/kənˈtɪnjuəl/`　*adj.*

> 故事义 反复出现的　·　常考义 连续的；频繁的

- **核心搭配**　`continual interruptions` 接二连三的打扰　·　`continual complaints` 频繁的抱怨　·　`continual improvement` 持续改进
- **近义替换**　repeated · frequent · constant
- **词族延伸**　continue v. → continual adj.
- **易混辨析**　continual 指「反复发生（有间断）」；continuous 指「一直不断（无间断）」，必分
- **加分句式**　Continual practice is the only road to mastery. 反复不断的练习是精通的唯一途径。

#### 26 · continue　`/kənˈtɪnjuː/`　*v.*

> 故事义 继续　·　常考义 继续（做）；持续

## 03 · The Displaced Community｜流离的群体

**题材** 科技 / 环境　　**收录** 13 词：dismiss · disorder · disperse · displace · display · distribute · distort · distract · evolution · evolve · exaggerate · examine · mock

### 英文故事

For twenty years Dr. Han had **examined** the coastline, and his maps told a plain story: the sea was rising. At town meetings he watched officials **dismiss** his charts with a smile, and the mayor once joked that a flood was a rumour invented to **distract** voters from real problems.

Then came the autumn storm. Water turned the main street into **disorder**, and the crowd that gathered to watch soon had to **disperse** for their lives. Forty families were **displaced** in a single night, their photographs and furniture piled on the steps of the museum where Han worked.

The next morning Han refused to **exaggerate**: "a century of neglect," he said, "not a century storm." Some reporters tried to **distort** that into panic; others used the flood to **mock** science itself. But inside the museum he put the ruined maps on **display** and invited students to examine the mud — the slow **evolution** of a coastline written in sediment. Watching volunteers **distribute** blankets, he let himself believe the town could **evolve** into something wiser.

## 04 · Urban Modernization｜都市的现代化

**题材** 教育 / 职场　　**收录** 14 词：mode · model · moderate · modern · modernization · modest · modify · predecessor · predict · preface · preferable · preference · prejudice · preliminary

### 英文故事

When the old textile mill announced its **modernization**, everyone expected a young engineer from the city. Instead they got Su, a woman of forty with a **modest** suitcase and no interest in speeches. Her task was to make the aging workshops **modern**; her **mode** of working was simple: walk the floor, listen, and change as little as possible.

The workers trusted no one; her **predecessor** had been a famous expert who never touched a machine. Su's **preliminary** report, when it came, ran to six pages, and its conclusion was that the old looms were **preferable** to any new **model** on the market. She asked only to **modify** three motors.

Her **moderate** proposal offended the salesmen, whose **prejudice** favoured whatever was newest and most expensive. "You cannot **predict** failure," they warned. Su disagreed — you cannot predict anything without data — and in the **preface** to her final report she thanked the oldest weaver by name. A year later the mill doubled its output, and the salesmen, with quiet grace, changed their **preference**.

- **加分句式**　Employers show a growing preference for practical experience. 雇主越来越偏爱实践经验。

#### 53 · prejudice　`/ˈpredʒudɪs/`　*n.*

> 故事义 偏见　·　常考义 偏见；成见

- **核心搭配**　`prejudice against` 对…的偏见　·　`without prejudice` 不带偏见　·　`racial prejudice` 种族偏见
- **近义替换**　bias · partiality · discrimination
- **词族延伸**　pre-（预先）+ judice（判断）；prejudiced adj. 有偏见的
- **易混辨析**　prejudice 是「先入为主的成见（多负面）」；bias 是「系统性偏向」，可中性
- **加分句式**　We should judge candidates on merit, free from prejudice. 我们应该不受偏见影响，凭实力评判候选人。

#### 54 · preliminary　`/prɪˈlɪmɪnəri/`　*adj.*

> 故事义 初步的　·　常考义 预备的；初步的

- **核心搭配**　`preliminary results` 初步结果　·　`a preliminary investigation` 初步调查　·　`preliminary talks` 预备会谈
- **近义替换**　initial · preparatory · tentative
- **词族延伸**　preliminarily adv.；prelim n. 预赛（口语）
- **易混辨析**　preliminary 指「正式步骤前的准备性」；primary 是「首要的」，同前缀不同义
- **加分句式**　Preliminary findings suggest the treatment is safe. 初步研究结果表明该疗法是安全的。

<details>
<summary><b>01 · The Approach｜新方法</b></summary>

林梅的第一篇论文被拒时，审稿人的意见只有一句：结论有意思，但证据站不住脚。这话很扎心，却也刺激她重新打开了数月前已经结束的实验。她拒绝让一次失败激起自我怀疑——相反，她想掀起一场真正的讨论。

期刊规定每论断都必须建立在公开数据之上，于是林梅花了数周清理数据表。她从噪声中开始推断出一种模式；这个推断虽然大胆，但一旦资料任人核查，就再也无法被忽视。连一位有影响力的批评者也回信了，承认这种新思路值得再看一眼。

「别让一件更差的工具遮住一个好问题，」那位批评者告诉她。在他的影响下，林梅重做了模型；而她的后续论文——正是那家期刊如今抢着刊出的那篇——证明了诚实的批评能让更好的科学得以传播。

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>02 · The Stamp of Approval｜认可的印记</b></summary>

作为能源委员会的助理，钱某意识到一个反复出现的抱怨：老电网撑不起持续的增长。国会春季会议开幕时，她起草了一份不算张扬的提案，要把每个村诊所接上稳定的电源——在她看来，电与生存之间的联系显而易见。

她的上司警告说，任何不符合预算的方案都永远得不到批准。与此同时，游说集团试图混淆公众视听，散布关于成本的种种混乱说法，直到连支持者都沉默下去。但钱某拒绝用愤怒正面对抗他们；她用一张电子表格回应每一条说法。

「唯一恰当的标准，是村子负担得起什么，」她对委员会说——这句贴切的话改变了投票。法案以广泛赞成获得通过，而那位难得认可任何事的主席，微笑着签了字。钱某继续盯着电表，她确信：政策像电力一样，必须走完最后一英里。

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>03 · The Displaced Community｜流离的群体</b></summary>

二十年来，韩博士一直在细查这条海岸线，他的地图讲着一个直白的故事：海平面在上升。在镇上的会议上，他看着官员们笑着对他的图表不予理会；市长甚至开玩笑说，洪水不过是为了转移选民对真正问题的注意而编造的谣言。

秋天的风暴来了。大水把主街变成一片混乱，闻讯赶来看热闹的人群很快不得不四散逃命。一夜之间，四十户人家流离失所，他们的照片和家具堆放在韩博士工作的博物馆台阶上。

第二天早上，韩博士拒绝夸大其词：「这是百年的忽视，」他说，「不是百年一遇的风暴。」有记者试图把他的话歪曲成恐慌渲染，也有人借这场洪水嘲笑科学本身。但在博物馆里，他把被毁的地图展出，请学生们细察那些淤泥——那是一部写在沉积物里的海岸线缓慢演变史。看着志愿者们分发毛毯，他让自己相信，这个镇子能演变成一个更明智的地方。

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>04 · Urban Modernization｜都市的现代化</b></summary>

老纺织厂宣布现代化改造时，所有人都以为会来个城里的年轻工程师。来的却是苏——一个四十岁、提着不大的行李箱、对讲话毫无兴趣的女人。她的任务是让老旧车间现代化；她的工作方式很简单：下车间走、听、尽量少改动。

工人们谁都不信；她的前任是个从不碰机器的名专家。苏的初步报告出来后只有六页，结论是：市场上的任何新式样都不如老织机可取。她只要求改装三台电机。

她这份温和适度的方案得罪了销售员——这些人的偏见是只认最新最贵的。「你预测不了失败，」他们警告说。苏不认同：没有数据，什么都预测不了。在最终报告的前言里，她点名感谢了厂里年纪最大的织工。一年后，厂子产量翻了一倍，而那些销售员也心平气和地改变了自己的偏好。

</details>
