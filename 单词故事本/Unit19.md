## 01 · The Premise｜前提

**题材** 学术 / 认知　　**收录** 16 词：premise · premium · preparation · reflect · reflection · reform · reject · rejoice · relate · relation · relationship · relative · relativity · relevant · overlook · overseas

### 英文故事

Dr. Han's **premise** was modest: that a school rises or falls on the quiet **preparation** of its teachers, not on the size of its budget. She had gathered much of her evidence from **overseas** systems that everyone praised and few had studied. Her grant application was **rejected** in eleven days; the reviewers found the argument interesting but not **relevant**.

She did not **rejoice** when a rival study confirmed her data; she asked what her own model had **overlooked**. The answer was **reflection** — not more data, but time for teachers to **reflect** on their own classrooms. What followed was a five-year **reform** of one poor district, documented with the patience of a physicist: every gain weighed with **relativity**, against what it had cost elsewhere.

The **relation** between funding and results, she wrote, is **relative** to trust; the **relationship** between teacher and student outweighs any curriculum. Only one official asked whether her findings **related** to city schools, and whether a **premium** should be paid to those who stayed longest. Her answer filled forty pages, and it opened with her premise again.

### 考点卡片

#### 01 · premise　`/ˈpremɪs/`　*n.*

> 故事义 前提　·　常考义 前提；假定

- **核心搭配**　`on the premise that...` 在…的前提下　·　`a false premise` 错误的前提　·　`business premises` 经营场所
- **近义替换**　assumption · supposition · hypothesis
- **词族延伸**　premise v. 预先提出 → premises n. 营业场所（僻义）
- **易混辨析**　premise 指论证推理所依据的假定；assumption 只是未经证明的想法，不一定进入论证链条
- **加分句式**　The plan rests on the premise that every child can learn, given time. 这个计划基于一个前提：只要给时间，每个孩子都能学会。

#### 02 · premium　`/ˈpriːmiəm/`　*n.*

> 故事义 额外津贴　·　常考义 奖金；保险费

- **核心搭配**　`pay a premium for` 为…支付溢价　·　`insurance premium` 保险费　·　`at a premium` 奇缺；以溢价
- **近义替换**　bonus · surcharge · insurance
- **词族延伸**　premia n. 复数形式
- **易混辨析**　premium 指「为更好之物多付的钱」或「保险费」；bonus 指额外奖金，与保险无关
- **加分句式**　Customers will pay a premium for honesty, and charge for its absence. 顾客愿意为诚实支付溢价，也会为它的缺席开出账单。

#### 03 · preparation　`/ˌprepəˈreɪʃn/`　*n.*

> 故事义 准备　·　常考义 准备

- **核心搭配**　`in preparation for` 为…作准备　·　`make preparations for` 为…作准备　·　`mental preparation` 心理准备
- **近义替换**　groundwork · readiness · arrangement
- **词族延伸**　prepare v. → preparation n. → preparatory adj. 预备的
- **易混辨析**　preparation 作不可数指「准备过程」；preparations 复数指具体的准备工作，如 make preparations
- **加分句式**　Luck is what preparation looks like from the outside. 运气，就是准备从外面看起来的样子。

#### 04 · reflect　`/rɪˈflekt/`　*v.*

> 故事义 反思　·　常考义 反映；考虑

- **核心搭配**　`reflect on / upon` 反思，仔细考虑　·　`reflect sth` 反映…　·　`be reflected in` 体现在…中
- **近义替换**　mirror · ponder · contemplate
- **词族延伸**　reflection n. → reflective adj. → reflector n. 反射器
- **易混辨析**　reflect on 表「反思」（须接 on/upon）；reflect 直接接宾语表「反映」——两义都是考点
- **加分句式**　A budget reflects values more honestly than any speech. 一份预算比任何演讲都更诚实地反映价值观。

#### 05 · reflection　`/rɪˈflekʃn/`　*n.*

> 故事义 反思　·　常考义 沉思

- **核心搭配**　`on reflection` 经再三考虑　·　`a reflection of …` 的反映　·　`be lost in reflection` 陷入沉思
- **近义替换**　contemplation · meditation · deliberation
- **词族延伸**　reflect v. → reflection n. → reflective adj. 沉思的
- **易混辨析**　reflection 作「倒影」可数，作「沉思」不可数；meditation 多指有系统的冥想练习
- **加分句式**　On reflection, she withdrew the question and kept the friendship. 再三考虑后，她收回了那个问题，保住了这段友谊。

#### 06 · reform　`/rɪˈfɔːm/`　*v. / n.*

> 故事义 改革　·　常考义 改革；改良；变革

- **核心搭配**　`reform the system` 改革制度　·　`economic reform` 经济改革　·　`reform oneself` 改过自新
- **近义替换**　overhaul · restructure · improve
- **词族延伸**　reformer n. 改革者 → reformation n. 改革
- **易混辨析**　reform 指在现有框架内改良；revolution 是推翻重建——改革的对象多为制度，revolution 针对政权
- **加分句式**　Reform succeeds when it changes incentives, not just rules. 改革改变的是激励而不只是条文时，才会成功。

#### 07 · reject　`/rɪˈdʒekt/`　*vt.*

> 故事义 驳回　·　常考义 拒绝；排斥

- **核心搭配**　`reject a proposal` 驳回提案　·　`reject sth outright` 断然拒绝　·　`be rejected by` 被…拒绝
- **近义替换**　refuse · decline · turn down
- **词族延伸**　rejection n. → rejected adj. → reject n. 次品
- **易混辨析**　reject 语气最硬，含「不采纳并退回」；decline 是婉拒；refuse 泛指拒绝
- **加分句式**　The journal rejected the paper, not the idea; the idea outlived the journal. 期刊退掉的是论文，不是想法；想法比期刊活得久。

#### 08 · rejoice　`/rɪˈdʒɔɪs/`　*v.*

> 故事义 庆幸　·　常考义 （使）感到高兴

- **核心搭配**　`rejoice at / over` 为…而高兴　·　`rejoice in` 因拥有…而欣喜　·　`rejoice to do` 因…而欢喜
- **近义替换**　celebrate · delight · exult
- **词族延伸**　rejoicing n. 欢庆
- **易混辨析**　rejoice 是带文学色彩的「欣喜」，正式而强烈；be glad 口语化且程度轻
- **加分句式**　We rejoiced at the news, then quietly went back to work. 我们为这个消息而欣喜，然后悄悄回到工作中。

#### 09 · relate　`/rɪˈleɪt/`　*v.*

> 故事义 适用于　·　常考义 讲述；叙述；使相互关联；联系

- **核心搭配**　`relate to` 与…相关；理解并认同　·　`relate sth to sb` 向某人讲述　·　`be related to` 与…有关
- **近义替换**　connect · narrate · associate
- **词族延伸**　relation n. → relationship n. → relative n. / adj.
- **易混辨析**　relate to 两考：「与…相关」与「理解、认同」（teenagers need adults they can relate to）
- **加分句式**　Readers relate to characters who hesitate, not to those who never doubt. 读者能与之共鸣的，是会犹豫的角色，而不是从不怀疑的角色。

#### 10 · relation　`/rɪˈleɪʃn/`　*n.*

> 故事义 关联　·　常考义 关系；联系

- **核心搭配**　`in relation to` 关于；与…相比　·　`bear no relation to` 与…完全不符　·　`diplomatic relations` 外交关系
- **近义替换**　connection · link · correlation
- **词族延伸**　relate v. → relation n. → relationship n.
- **易混辨析**　relation 较抽象、正式；relationship 多指人际或具体关联；relations 复数常指「邦交、交往」
- **加分句式**　Price bears little relation to quality in markets where trust is scarce. 在信任稀缺的市场里，价格与质量关系不大。

#### 11 · relationship　`/rɪˈleɪʃnʃɪp/`　*n.*

> 故事义 师生关系　·　常考义 关系；联系

- **核心搭配**　`a close relationship with` 与…的亲密关系　·　`build a relationship` 建立关系　·　`working relationship` 工作关系
- **近义替换**　bond · tie · connection
- **词族延伸**　relate → relation → relationship n. 相关延伸
- **易混辨析**　relationship 指人际间持续的情感或工作联系；relation 更抽象正式，常用复数指外交关系
- **加分句式**　The best relationships between teachers and students are built on respect, not fear. 最好的师生关系建立在尊重之上，而非恐惧。

#### 12 · relative　`/ˈrelətɪv/`　*n. / adj.*

> 故事义 相对的　·　常考义 亲戚；比较的；相对的

- **核心搭配**　`a close relative` 近亲　·　`relative to` 相对于　·　`relative advantage` 相对优势
- **近义替换**　comparative · proportional · kin n.
- **词族延伸**　relate → relative n. / adj. → relatively adv.
- **易混辨析**　relative 作名词是「亲戚」，作形容词「相对的」常与 to 连用；relevant 是「相关的」，形近义异
- **加分句式**　Success is relative to the obstacles you started with. 成功是相对于你出发时面对的障碍而言的。

#### 13 · relativity　`/ˌreləˈtɪvəti/`　*n.*

> 故事义 相对性　·　常考义 相关（性）；相对论

- **核心搭配**　`the theory of relativity` 相对论　·　`relativity of values` 价值观的相对性　·　`general / special relativity` 广义 / 狭义相对论
- **近义替换**　relativism n. 相对主义 · correlation
- **词族延伸**　relative adj. → relativity n.
- **易混辨析**　relativity 物理上指爱因斯坦「相对论」，一般语境指「相对性」；reality（现实）形近音近，最易混
- **加分句式**　Einstein's relativity teaches that even time depends on where you stand. 爱因斯坦的相对论告诉我们，连时间也取决于你所站的立场。

#### 14 · relevant　`/ˈreləvənt/`　*adj.*

> 故事义 切题的　·　常考义 有关的；切题的；相关联的；有意义的

- **核心搭配**　`be relevant to` 与…相关　·　`relevant experience` 相关经验　·　`highly relevant` 高度相关
- **近义替换**　pertinent · applicable · germane
- **词族延伸**　relevance n. → relevantly adv. → irrelevant adj. 无关的
- **易混辨析**　relevant 指「与当前问题相关、切题」；relative 指「相对的」——二者只差一个字母，最易混
- **加分句式**　The question is not whether the data are new, but whether they are relevant. 问题不在于数据是否新颖，而在于它们是否切题。

#### 15 · overlook　`/ˌəʊvəˈlʊk/`　*vt.*

> 故事义 忽略　·　常考义 看漏；忽略

- **核心搭配**　`overlook a detail` 忽略细节　·　`overlook the sea` 俯瞰大海　·　`be easily overlooked` 容易被忽视
- **近义替换**　neglect · miss · disregard
- **词族延伸**　over + look → overlooked adj.
- **易混辨析**　overlook 兼「俯瞰」（本义）与「忽视」（引申，多指无心之失）；ignore 是「故意不理睬」
- **加分句式**　In praising the reform, the report overlooked the teachers who made it work. 报告在称赞改革时，忽视了让它落地的教师们。

#### 16 · overseas　`/ˌəʊvəˈsiːz/`　*adj. / adv.*

> 故事义 海外　·　常考义 外国的；海外的；在海外；在国外

- **核心搭配**　`overseas students` 留学生　·　`study overseas` 在海外求学　·　`overseas markets` 海外市场
- **近义替换**　abroad · foreign · international
- **词族延伸**　over + sea(s) → overseas adj. / adv.
- **易混辨析**　overseas 可兼形容词与副词（overseas students / go overseas）；abroad 只能作副词，不能说 an abroad student
- **加分句式**　Experience gained overseas often reads as a threat at home. 在海外获得的阅历，在本土常被读作一种威胁。

## 02 · The Statute｜法令

**题材** 法律 / 社会　　**收录** 20 词：stand · standard · standpoint · stance · start · startle · starve · state · statement · statesman · statistics · statue · status · statute · stay · steady · steer · stem · step · stern

### 英文故事

The famine began quietly, as famines do: first the **statistics** bent, then the markets. In the capital a **statesman** old enough to remember two wars stood before parliament and read a single **statement** — the **state** would guarantee a minimum **standard** of food for every citizen. Outside, rain streaked the **statue** of the republic's founder, who had **starved** as a young officer and never forgotten it.

The **statute** that followed ran to forty pages and was written in the dry voice of lawyers. From every **standpoint** it was imperfect: farmers said the price was too low, merchants said the targets too high, and the treasury, whose **status** had just been downgraded, said both. The old man took a firm **stance** — compromise now, refinement later — and dared the opposition to **stand** against bread. His allies called him **stern**; his enemies called him slow.

The programme did not **start** with grain but with salt and cooking oil, and that startled no one; what **startled** the capital was where the riots stopped. Step by **step** it moved — county granaries first, then city queues — each one kept **steady** by clerks who had learned to **steer** panic into patience. Families chose to **stay**, and the exodus began to **stem**. By winter the law's defenders could stand on street corners without guards, and the old statesman, visiting the founder's statue in the snow, said nothing at all.

### 考点卡片

#### 17 · stand　`/stænd/`　*v. / n.*

> 故事义 顶住　·　常考义 站立；位于；维持现状；忍受；经受

- **核心搭配**　`stand for` 代表　·　`stand up for` 维护　·　`stand by` 支持；袖手旁观　·　`cannot stand doing` 无法忍受
- **近义替换**　tolerate · represent · withstand
- **词族延伸**　stood（过去式）→ standing n. 地位 → standpoint n. 立场
- **易混辨析**　stand 表「忍受」只用于否定与疑问（can't stand）；表「代表」须用 stand for
- **加分句式**　Stand for something, or you will fall for anything. 有所坚守，否则你会对什么都信以为真。

#### 18 · standard　`/ˈstændəd/`　*n. / adj.*

> 故事义 标准　·　常考义 标准；规格；规范的

- **核心搭配**　`meet the standard` 达到标准　·　`a high standard of` 高水准的…　·　`standard of living` 生活水平　·　`up to standard` 合乎标准
- **近义替换**　criterion · benchmark · norm
- **词族延伸**　standardize v. → standardization n.
- **易混辨析**　standard 指公认的衡量尺度；criterion 是评判的「准则」，更正式，复数为 criteria
- **加分句式**　The standard you walk past is the standard you accept. 你走过去的那个标准，就是你接受的那个标准。

#### 19 · standpoint　`/ˈstændpɔɪnt/`　*n.*

> 故事义 立场　·　常考义 立场；观点

- **核心搭配**　`from a... standpoint` 从…的角度来看　·　`from the standpoint of` 从…的立场出发　·　`a historical standpoint` 历史视角
- **近义替换**　perspective · viewpoint · angle
- **词族延伸**　stand + point → standpoint n.
- **易混辨析**　standpoint 强调持论者所处的位置，常与 from 连用；viewpoint 泛指看法，口语色彩更浓
- **加分句式**　From an economic standpoint, the policy was sound; from a human one, it failed. 从经济角度看，这项政策无可挑剔；从人的角度看，它是失败的。

#### 20 · stance　`/stæns/`　*n.*

> 故事义 立场　·　常考义 观点；态度；立场

- **核心搭配**　`take a stance on` 对…表明立场　·　`a firm stance` 坚定的立场　·　`a neutral stance` 中立态度
- **近义替换**　position · posture · attitude
- **词族延伸**　stand → stance n.（同源名词）
- **易混辨析**　stance 是公开表明的立场态度，仍带身体姿势的联想；standpoint 是看问题的出发点
- **加分句式**　The minister took a firm stance: reform now, refinement later. 部长表明了坚定立场：先改革，再完善。

#### 21 · start　`/stɑːt/`　*v. / n.*

> 故事义 开始　·　常考义 开始；动身；启动；开端

- **核心搭配**　`start with` 从…开始　·　`start from scratch` 从零开始　·　`make a start` 开始动手　·　`to start with` 首先
- **近义替换**　begin · commence · launch
- **词族延伸**　starter n. 起跑者；开胃菜 → startle v. 惊吓（同源）
- **易混辨析**　start 与 begin 近义；commence 正式，多用于典礼与法律程序；start 还可指「动身、启动机器」
- **加分句式**　Every long journey starts with a decision to take the first step. 每段长途都始于一个「迈出第一步」的决定。

#### 22 · startle　`/ˈstɑːtl/`　*v.*

> 故事义 使吃惊　·　常考义 惊吓；使吃惊

- **核心搭配**　`be startled by / at` 被…吓了一跳　·　`a startling discovery` 惊人的发现　·　`startle sb out of sleep` 把某人从睡梦中惊醒
- **近义替换**　frighten · scare · shock
- **词族延伸**　start v. → startle v. → startling adj. 惊人的
- **易混辨析**　startle 指突然的短促惊吓；frighten 程度更深更持久；astonish 侧重「难以置信」而非恐惧
- **加分句式**　What startled the committee was not the cost, but the silence of the experts. 让委员会吃惊的不是成本，而是专家们的沉默。

#### 23 · starve　`/stɑːv/`　*v.*

> 故事义 挨饿　·　常考义 （使）挨饿；（使）饿死

- **核心搭配**　`starve to death` 饿死　·　`be starved of` 极度缺乏…　·　`starve sb into submission` 以断粮逼人就范
- **近义替换**　famish · hunger · deprive
- **词族延伸**　starvation n. → starving adj. → starveling n. 饿瘦的人
- **易混辨析**　starve 本义「饿死」；be starved of 是高频引申，指「极度缺乏」（starved of funds）
- **加分句式**　A budget starved of maintenance is a debt sold to the future. 维护经费被抽空的预算，是一笔赊给未来的债。

#### 24 · state　`/steɪt/`　*n. / v.*

> 故事义 国家　·　常考义 状态；情况；国；州；陈述；说明

- **核心搭配**　`state clearly` 明确说明　·　`in a... state` 处于…状态　·　`the state` 国家；政府　·　`state of mind` 心态
- **近义替换**　condition n. · declare v. · nation n.
- **词族延伸**　statement n. → statesman n. → statehood n. 州地位

## 03 · The Assembly｜集会

**题材** 法律 / 社会　　**收录** 17 词：anonymous · answer · anticipate · anxiety · anxious · apart · assassinate · assault · assemble · assembly · assert · component · compose · composition · comprehend · comprehension · comprehensive

### 英文故事

The warning arrived at dawn: an **anonymous** letter, posted three days earlier, saying only that the square would remember that Friday. The police did not **anticipate** a real threat — public figures had weathered worse — and by noon a crowd began to **assemble** for the weekly **assembly** of the reform movement. The violence lasted four seconds: a man drew a pistol, a bodyguard moved, and what was meant to **assassinate** a minister became, on ten thousand phone screens, an **assault** upon the idea of assembly itself.

In the aftermath the city lived on a diet of **anxiety** and rumour. Families **anxious** about the curfew were held **apart** at the checkpoints, and hospitals asked visitors not to **assert** kinships they could not prove. The one thing everyone shared was the need to **comprehend** what had happened; the one thing no one could **compose** was a sentence equal to it.

Months later the inquiry released its **comprehensive** report: four hundred pages on every **component** of the failure, from the lost letter to the unguarded gate. Its central **composition**, a chapter titled The Week Before, read like a novel and indicted like a verdict. The **answer** to why the letter had been ignored, the report concluded, was the oldest in public life: nobody wanted it to be true. The city read it in silence — a kind of understanding, if not yet full **comprehension**.

## 04 · The Conductor｜指挥家

**题材** 教育 / 职场　　**收录** 12 词：condemn · condense · condition · conduct · conductor · disable · disappear · disappoint · discipline · disclose · discover · discovery

### 英文故事

When the **conductor** Wei An agreed to **conduct** the provincial orchestra, he found the hall in a **condition** his students would have failed: roof cracked, scores mouldering, the rehearsal regime a museum of old habits. The players had talent but no **discipline**; the board had opinions but no money. He accepted anyway, on one condition — that nobody call the task impossible within his hearing.

Auditing the accounts, the new manager made a **discovery** that would **disappoint** the board's chairman: a decade of 'emergency loans' had **disappeared** into a private fund. She did not **disclose** it at once; she let the auditors **discover** it themselves, then read the findings aloud at the meeting. The board voted to **condemn** the practice and hand it to the prosecutor. One member warned that the orchestra itself would be tainted. Wei replied that silence was the worse stain.

That winter an icy road nearly **disabled** the concertmaster's left hand, and for a week the season seemed finished. Wei refused to cancel. He **condensed** the programme to five works, hauled the hall's piano onto the stage himself, and told the audience the plain truth on opening night. They came anyway, filling a half-repaired hall; nobody left early, nobody asked for a refund. As one critic wrote, the orchestra had learned to play the condition it was in.

## 05 · The Overlook｜瞭望处

**题材** 社会 / 环境　　**收录** 17 词：hoist · hold · holder · household · however · huddle · narrative · narrow · nasty · nation · national · nationality · native · natural · nature · overall · overcome

### 英文故事

The typhoon made landfall at three in the morning, and the fishing village of Lankou met it the way it had met every storm for four centuries: every **household** up on the hill, doors **held** fast from inside, whole families in one **huddle** listening to the roof. By dawn, helicopters were crossing the strait to **hoist** the old and the young to safety while the able-bodied stayed to hold the nets and the boats. The village head, **holder** of the communal fishing licence for thirty years, stood at the jetty counting heads in the rain.

The **national** news called it a **natural** disaster — the phrase a **nation** reaches for when the wind is the only culprit it can name. The village told a different **narrative**: mangroves cleared for a resort, a warning buoy left unmoored, a shipping channel cut **narrow** where the old maps marked a wide one. **However** the official report finally phrased it, the **native** families knew which part was **nature** and which part was choice. Families whose **nationality** no one questioned waited on compensation for land their grandmothers had planted. The argument, when it came, turned **nasty** within a week.

The **overall** picture took a year to assemble: eleven homes gone, the school saved, the boats sunk. The village did not overcome the storm — no one overcomes a storm — but it **overcame** the settlement, the silence, and the story that had set it apart. The rebuilt school now teaches the old channel names alongside the national curriculum, and on clear mornings the children climb the ridge where, their grandmothers say, you can watch the sea decide.

- **易混辨析**　nation 强调民族共同体、国民整体；country 侧重国土；state 侧重政权、政体
- **加分句式**　A nation's character is tested by how it treats those who cannot vote. 一个国家的品格，体现在它如何对待没有选票的人。

#### 76 · national　`/ˈnæʃnəl/`　*adj.*

> 故事义 国家的　·　常考义 民族的；国家的

- **核心搭配**　`national security` 国家安全　·　`a national park` 国家公园　·　`national identity` 民族认同　·　`national economy` 国民经济

- **易混辨析**　national 兼「国家的」与「民族的」两义，national identity 常指后者；nationwide 只强调全国范围

<details>
<summary><b>01 · The Premise｜前提</b></summary>

韩博士的前提很朴素：一所学校的兴衰，取决于教师平日不显眼的准备，而非预算的规模。她的证据多来自海外那些人人称赞、却少有人研究的体系。拨款申请十一天就被驳回了，评审意见是：论点有意思，但不切题。

当对手的研究证实了她的数据时，她并没有庆幸，而是追问自己的模型忽略了什么。答案是反思——不是更多的数据，而是让教师回望自己课堂的时间。此后是五年对一个贫困学区的改革，记录方式带着物理学家般的耐心：每一项收获都用相对性来衡量，对照它在别处付出的代价。

她写道，经费与成效之间的关联是相对于信任而言的；师生关系胜过任何课程表。只有一位官员追问：她的发现是否也适用于城市学校？是否该给那些坚守最久的教师发放额外津贴。她的答复写了四十页，开头仍是那个前提。

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>02 · The Statute｜法令</b></summary>

饥荒总是静悄悄地开始：先是统计数字弯了下去，然后是市场。首都一位经历过两场战争的老政治家站到议会前，宣读了一份简短的声明——国家将向每一位公民保障最低的食物标准。议会外，雨水划过共和国奠基者的雕像；他年轻时挨过饿，一生未曾忘记。

随后出台的法令长达四十页，通篇是律师式的干巴巴措辞。从任何立场看，它都不完美：农民说收购价太低，商人说指标太高，刚被下调评级的财政部则说两者都对。老人态度坚定——先妥协，后完善——并放话让反对派来反对面包试试。盟友称他严厉，对手称他迟缓。

这项计划最先配发的不是粮食，而是盐和食用油，这没有让任何人吃惊；让首都吃惊的是骚乱停下的地方。它一步一步推进——先是县城粮库，然后是城市排队点——每一步都靠一群学会了把恐慌疏导成耐心的职员维持平稳。一家人家选择留下，外流的势头开始被遏制。到了冬天，法案的捍卫者已经不用卫兵就能站在街角；而老政治家踏着雪去看奠基者的雕像，一言未发。

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>03 · The Assembly｜集会</b></summary>

警告在黎明时送到：一封三天前寄出的匿名信，只说「这个广场会记住那个星期五」。警方没有料到真正的威胁——公众人物经历过的风浪多了——中午前，人群开始聚集，参加改革运动每周的集会。暴力只持续了四秒：一名男子拔出手枪，一名保镖迎上去；一场原本意在暗杀部长的行动，在一万块手机屏幕里，变成对整个集会理念的袭击。

事件之后，这座城市靠焦虑和谣言度日。为宵禁担心的家庭在检查站被分隔开来，医院则要求探视者不要声称无法证明的亲属关系。所有人共同的需要是理解发生了什么；而没有人写得出一个配得上它的句子。

数月后，调查组公布了全面的报告：四百页篇幅，写尽失败链条上的每一个环节——从那封丢失的信，到无人看守的大门。它的核心章节《前一周》写得像小说，定罪却像判决书。报告总结道：那封信为何被无视，答案出自公共生活里最古老的那一条——没有人愿意它是真的。全城沉默地读完了它：这是一种理解，尽管还算不上彻底的理解。

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>04 · The Conductor｜指挥家</b></summary>

指挥家韦安答应执掌省交响乐团时，发现音乐厅的状况放到他的学生手里都不及格：屋顶开裂、乐谱发霉、排练制度像一座陈列旧习惯的博物馆。乐手有天赋却没有纪律；理事会有意见却没有钱。他还是答应了，只提了一个条件——不许当着他的面说这件事不可能。

新任经理查账时有一个发现，它注定要让理事会主席失望：十年的「紧急借款」竟消失在一个私人账户里。她没有立刻公开，而是让审计师自己查了出来，再在会议上当众宣读。理事会投票谴责这种做法，并将其移交给检方。一名成员警告说，乐团本身也会蒙上污点。韦安答道：沉默才是更脏的污点。

那年冬天，一条结冰的路几乎让首席小提琴手的左手致残，整整一周，演出季眼看要泡汤。韦安拒绝取消。他把曲目压缩到五部，亲自把厅里的钢琴搬上舞台，并在首演之夜对观众说了大实话。人们还是来了，坐满了一座修了一半的音乐厅；没有人提前离场，没有人要求退票。一位乐评人写道：这支乐团学会了在所处的境况中演奏。

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>05 · The Overlook｜瞭望处</b></summary>

台风在凌晨三点登陆，渔村兰口以四百年来的老办法迎接它：每一户人家都上了高地，从里面抵紧家门，全家人挤作一团，听着屋顶作响。天亮时，直升机正越过海峡，把老人和孩子吊运到安全处，能干活的人则留下守网守船。当了三十年渔业执照持有人的村长站在码头上，淋着雨点人头。

国家新闻把它称为自然灾害——这是一个国家在唯一能指认的元凶是风时伸手就能拿到的措辞。村子讲的是另一个版本：为建度假村清掉的红树林、没人固定的警示浮标、在老地图上本该宽阔处被挖窄的航道。无论官方报告最后如何措辞，本地家庭都清楚哪一部分是大自然，哪一部分是人为选择。国籍无人质疑的家庭，在等一块祖辈种过的地的补偿款。争论到来后，一周内就变得难听。

总体图景花了一年才拼齐：十一栋房屋没了，学校保住了，船沉了。村子没有战胜风暴——没人能战胜风暴——但它克服了那份和解方案、那段沉默，以及那个把自己说成另类的故事。重建的学校如今在国家课程之外还教老航道的名字；晴朗的早晨，孩子们爬上祖母们说的那条山脊——在那里，你能看着大海拿主意。

</details>
