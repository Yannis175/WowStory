## 01 · The Charter｜章程

**题材** 社会 / 法律　　**收录** 16 词：portion · possibility · possible · potential · pour · console · consist · consistent · constant · constituent · constitute · constitution · constrain · construct · distinct · distinction

### 英文故事

The rain began to **pour** on the second night of the flood, and by morning half the town's fields lay under water. Every family queued for its **portion** of relief grain, and the **possibility** of a second flood was no longer a remote one. Mayor Chen walked the shelter rows herself, stopping to **console** every family that had lost a home. The **potential** for disease, the doctors warned, was real.

On the tenth day the council voted to **constitute** a reconstruction committee of seven — one delegate from each **constituent** district — and gave it one year to draft a town **constitution**. The document would **consist** of twelve clauses: budgets must be **consistent** with the relief plan, audits must be **constant**, and no emergency decree could **constrain** the press. It was **possible**, Chen admitted, that they would fail. But a town, she said, is not rebuilt with tents; it is rebuilt with rules.

Before the vote, the committee had to **construct** a drainage system out of almost nothing, and to draw a hard **distinction** between emergency powers and ordinary ones. The difference between the old administration and the new one was **distinct** to every reader of the charter. When the constitution passed, 214 to 9, Chen did not smile. She was already looking at the river.

### 考点卡片

#### 01 · portion　`/ˈpɔːʃn/`　*n. / v.*

> 故事义 一份　·　常考义 （一）部分

- **核心搭配**　`a portion of` 一部分；一份　·　`divide sth into portions` 把…分成若干份　·　`a generous portion` 一大份
- **近义替换**　part · segment · share
- **词族延伸**　portion v. 分配（与名词同形）
- **易混辨析**　portion 强调「从整体中分出的一份」，常接 of；part 是最通用的「部分」，无分份意味
- **加分句式**　The first portion of any budget should go to the people it is meant to serve. 任何预算的第一份，都应花在它本该服务的人身上。

#### 02 · possibility　`/ˌpɒsəˈbɪləti/`　*n.*

> 故事义 可能性　·　常考义 可能；可能性；可能的事；希望

- **核心搭配**　`the possibility of doing sth` 做某事的可能性　·　`a real / distinct possibility` 现实的可能性　·　`raise the possibility of` 提出…的可能　·　`within the bounds of possibility` 在可能的范围内
- **近义替换**　likelihood · potential · prospect
- **词族延伸**　possible adj. → possibly adv. → possibility n.
- **易混辨析**　possibility 只是「可能存在」，不含概率大小；probability 才强调「概率」，可能性更大
- **加分句式**　Every reform begins with the possibility that things could be otherwise. 每一项改革，都始于「事情本可以是另一个样子」的可能性。

#### 03 · possible　`/ˈpɒsəbl/`　*adj.*

> 故事义 可能的　·　常考义 可能的；合理的

- **核心搭配**　`as soon as possible` 尽快　·　`it is possible that...` 有可能…　·　`do everything possible` 尽一切可能　·　`if possible` 如果可能的话
- **近义替换**　feasible · achievable · viable
- **词族延伸**　possibly adv. 可能地 → possibility n. 可能性 → impossible adj. 不可能的
- **易混辨析**　possible 只陈述「可能发生」；probable 表示「很可能发生」，语气更强
- **加分句式**　It is possible to respect a decision while still questioning the process behind it. 尊重一个决定，同时质疑它背后的程序，这两件事可以并存。

#### 04 · potential　`/pəˈtenʃl/`　*n. / adj.*

> 故事义 潜在风险　·　常考义 可能性；潜力；潜能；潜在的；可能的

- **核心搭配**　`the potential for sth …` 的潜在可能　·　`reach one's full potential` 充分发挥潜能　·　`a potential customer` 潜在客户　·　`have the potential to do` 有做…的潜力
- **近义替换**　latent · prospective · promising
- **词族延伸**　potentially adv. 潜在地 → potency n. 潜力；效力
- **易混辨析**　potential 作名词指「尚未兑现的潜能」，作形容词指「潜在的」；possible 只表「可能」，不强调「蕴含力量」
- **加分句式**　Every crisis carries the potential for change, but never the guarantee of it. 每一场危机都蕴含变革的可能，却从不保证变革必然到来。

#### 05 · pour　`/pɔː(r)/`　*v.*

> 故事义 倾盆而下　·　常考义 下倾盆大雨；倾泻

- **核心搭配**　`pour sth into` 把…倒入　·　`pour down` 倾盆而下　·　`pour money into` 向…大笔投入资金　·　`pour out` 倾诉；涌出
- **近义替换**　stream · flow · heap 倒
- **词族延伸**　pouring adj. 倾盆的（pouring rain）
- **易混辨析**　pour 强调「大量、快速地倾注」，可指液体、金钱、人群；flow 只强调「持续流动」，不含量多
- **加分句式**　Public money should be poured into schools before it is poured into stadiums. 公共资金该先倾注于学校，再谈倾注于体育馆。

#### 06 · console　`/kənˈsəʊl/`　*vt. / n.*

> 故事义 安慰　·　常考义 安慰；慰问；控制台

- **核心搭配**　`console sb for sth` 因某事安慰某人　·　`console oneself with` 以…自我宽慰　·　`a games console` 游戏机
- **近义替换**　comfort · soothe · solace
- **词族延伸**　consolation n. 安慰 → consolatory adj. 安慰的
- **易混辨析**　console 是「让痛苦者好受一些」，对象多为他人；comfort 兼指「身体上的舒适」，词义更宽
- **加分句式**　Statistics cannot console a family; only the truth, spoken plainly, can. 数字安慰不了一个家庭；只有Plainly说出的真相可以。

#### 07 · consist　`/kənˈsɪst/`　*vi.*

> 故事义 包含　·　常考义 组成；在于；存在于

- **核心搭配**　`consist of` 由…组成　·　`consist in` 在于，存在于　·　`consist with` 与…一致
- **近义替换**　comprise · be made up of · lie in 在于
- **词族延伸**　consistent adj. 一致的 → consistency n. 一致性；稠度
- **易混辨析**　consist of 表「由…构成」（整体在前）；consist in 表「在于」（本质在前）；consist with 现已罕用，多用 be consistent with
- **加分句式**　Good governance consists of rules that bind the rulers as well as the ruled. 良治在于一套既约束被治理者、也约束治理者自身的规则。

#### 08 · consistent　`/kənˈsɪstənt/`　*adj.*

> 故事义 一致的　·　常考义 始终如一的；连续的；持续的；与…一致的

- **核心搭配**　`be consistent with` 与…一致　·　`a consistent policy` 一以贯之的政策　·　`consistent growth` 持续的增长　·　`remain consistent` 保持前后一致
- **近义替换**　coherent · steadfast · constant
- **词族延伸**　consist vi. → consistency n. → consistently adv.
- **易混辨析**　consistent 强调「前后一致、不自相矛盾」；constant 强调「持续不变」。两者常同时出现在选项中
- **加分句式**　A consistent policy is worth more than a brilliant one that changes every year. 一项前后一致的政策，胜过一项年年变动的聪明政策。

#### 09 · constant　`/ˈkɒnstənt/`　*adj.*

> 故事义 常态化的　·　常考义 永恒的；经常的；不断的

- **核心搭配**　`constant pressure` 持续的压力　·　`at a constant speed` 以恒定的速度　·　`constant exposure to` 长期暴露在…　·　`a constant companion` 忠实的伙伴
- **近义替换**　continual · persistent · steady
- **词族延伸**　constantly adv. 不断地 → constancy n. 恒定；坚贞
- **易混辨析**　constant 强调「稳定、不变、持续」；continual 强调「一再重复」，常含厌烦意味
- **加分句式**　Trust is the constant audit that no institution can outsource. 信任是一场常态化的审计，任何机构都外包不了。

#### 10 · constituent　`/kənˈstɪtjuənt/`　*adj. / n.*

> 故事义 构成选区的　·　常考义 构成的；组成的；成分；选民

- **核心搭配**　`a constituent part` 组成部分　·　`the constituents of …` 的选民　·　`constituent assembly` 制宪会议
- **近义替换**　component · elector · voter
- **词族延伸**　constitute v. 组成 → constitution n. 宪法
- **易混辨析**　constituent 作名词兼「成分」与「选民」两义；constitution 是「宪法」，词形相近义更远
- **加分句式**　A representative who ignores his constituents will soon have none left to represent. 一个无视选民的代表，很快就没有人可代表了。

#### 11 · constitute　`/ˈkɒnstɪtjuːt/`　*v.*

> 故事义 成立　·　常考义 组成；构成

- **核心搭配**　`constitute a committee` 成立委员会　·　`constitute a threat to` 对…构成威胁　·　`constitute 80% of` 占…的八成
- **近义替换**　form · establish · amount to
- **词族延伸**　constituent adj./n. → constitution n. 宪法 → constitutional adj. 宪法的
- **易混辨析**　constitute 是「整体由部分构成」的正式说法（Seven members constitute the committee）；consist of 则倒装（The committee consists of seven members）
- **加分句式**　Seven people with a shared rule can constitute a government; one person with an army can only imitate one. 七个人守着共同的规则可以成立一个政府；一个人带着军队，只能模仿一个政府。

#### 12 · constitution　`/ˌkɒnstɪˈtjuːʃn/`　*n.*

> 故事义 宪章　·　常考义 宪法；章程；体质；体格

- **核心搭配**　`draft a constitution` 起草宪法　·　`amend the constitution` 修宪　·　`a written constitution` 成文宪法　·　`a strong constitution` 强健的体格
- **近义替换**　charter · statute · make-up 构成
- **词族延伸**　constitute v. → constitutional adj. → constitutionally adv.
- **易混辨析**　constitution 兼「宪法」「构造」「体质」三义；charter 只指「特许状、章程」，无法律根本法地位
- **加分句式**　A constitution is not what a nation says on paper but what it does on its worst day. 宪法不是一个民族纸面上写了什么，而是它在最糟的那一天做了什么。

#### 13 · constrain　`/kənˈstreɪn/`　*vt.*

> 故事义 限制　·　常考义 限制；约束

- **核心搭配**　`constrain sb to do sth` 迫使某人做某事　·　`be constrained by` 受…的约束　·　`constrain spending` 压缩开支　·　`feel constrained to` 感到不得不
- **近义替换**　restrict · compel · restrain
- **词族延伸**　constraint n. 约束；限制因素 → constrained adj. 受约束的
- **易混辨析**　constrain 强调「外力约束、迫使」，可接 sb to do；restrain 强调「自我克制」（restrain oneself），一般不接 to do
- **加分句式**　A free press is constrained by facts, not by decrees. 自由的新闻界受事实约束，而不受法令约束。

#### 14 · construct　`/kənˈstrʌkt/`　*v. / n.*

> 故事义 建造　·　常考义 建造；创立；构想；概（观）念

- **核心搭配**　`construct a bridge` 架桥　·　`construct an argument` 构建论点　·　`a theoretical construct` 理论构想　·　`be constructed from` 由…建成
- **近义替换**　build · erect · devise
- **词族延伸**　construction n. 建造；建筑物 → constructive adj. 建设性的 → reconstruct v. 重建
- **易混辨析**　construct 的名词是 construction，注意与 contrast（对比）只差一两个字母，形近易混
- **加分句式**　It is easier to construct a new system than to repair trust in an old one. 搭建一个新体系，比修复对一个旧体系的信任要容易。

#### 15 · distinct　`/dɪˈstɪŋkt/`　*adj.*

> 故事义 分明的　·　常考义 不同的；明显的；清楚的

- **核心搭配**　`be distinct from` 与…截然不同　·　`a distinct possibility` 现实的可能　·　`a distinct advantage` 明显的优势　·　`in distinct categories` 界限分明的类别
- **近义替换**　different · clear · definite
- **词族延伸**　distinction n. 差别；卓越 → distinctive adj. 有特色的 → distinctly adv.
- **易混辨析**　distinct 强调「可以清楚区分开」；distinctive 强调「有独特风格的」。a distinct smell 是「明显的气味」，a distinctive smell 是「标志性的气味」
- **加分句式**　There is a distinct difference between refusing to answer and having nothing to say. 拒绝回答与无话可说，是截然不同的两件事。

#### 16 · distinction　`/dɪˈstɪŋkʃn/`　*n.*

> 故事义 界限　·　常考义 差别；区分；优秀；卓越

- **核心搭配**　`draw a distinction between` 在…之间划清界限　·　`make no distinction` 一视同仁　·　`a writer of distinction` 杰出的作家　·　`with distinction` 以优异成绩
- **近义替换**　difference · contrast · eminence
- **词族延伸**　distinct adj. → distinction n. → distinctive adj.
- **易混辨析**　distinction 兼「差别」与「卓越」两义；difference 只有「差别」一义
- **加分句式**　The law must draw a distinction between error and deceit, or punish both as if they were the same. 法律必须在错误与欺骗之间划出界限，否则便会把两者混为一谈一并惩罚。

## 02 · The Evidence｜证据

**题材** 社会 / 商业　　**收录** 16 词：distinguish · fruitful · frustrate · fulfill · function · fund · fundamental · evidence · evident · evil · environment · enterprise · entertain · entertainment · monopoly · monotonous

### 英文故事

For four years Gao cut trailers at Aurora, an **entertainment** **enterprise** that had grown into a **monopoly** without anyone quite noticing. Her own job was **monotonous** — the same three-second transitions, the same hollow music — and the **environment** on the ninth floor discouraged questions. The company's official **function**, according to its annual letter, was to **entertain** two hundred million people. Its real function, Gao suspected, was to keep them from counting.

The **evidence** arrived by accident: a backup drive left in a drawer. It took her three months to **distinguish** the genuine viewing figures from the ones the sales team had invented, and the pattern, once seen, was **evident** — fake numbers were used to **fund** new acquisitions. Her lawyer called it fraud; her mother called it courage. Gao called it the **fundamental** lie at the heart of every confident chart.

The lawsuit would **frustrate** the company's expansion for years, and no settlement could **fulfill** what the shareholders had been promised. In the end Gao refused to call the company **evil** — evil, she said, requires intent, and what she had found was only greed wearing a smile. Her patience proved **fruitful**: the monopoly was broken into three. She spent the bonus on a small garden, and has not opened the streaming app since.

### 考点卡片

#### 17 · distinguish　`/dɪˈstɪŋɡwɪʃ/`　*v.*

> 故事义 辨别　·　常考义 区别；辨别；使出众

- **核心搭配**　`distinguish A from B` 区分 A 与 B　·　`distinguish between right and wrong` 明辨是非　·　`distinguish oneself` 使自己出名　·　`distinguish fact from opinion` 区分事实与观点
- **近义替换**　differentiate · discriminate · tell apart
- **词族延伸**　distinguished adj. 杰出的 → distinguishable adj. 可区分的
- **易混辨析**　distinguish 是中性词「辨别」；discriminate 除「辨别」外还常指「歧视」，语境敏感
- **加分句式**　The first skill of a scientist is to distinguish what is known from what is merely believed. 科学家的第一项本领，是分清「已知」与「只是相信」。

#### 18 · fruitful　`/ˈfruːtfl/`　*adj.*

> 故事义 有回报的　·　常考义 富有成效的

- **核心搭配**　`a fruitful discussion` 富有成果的会谈　·　`prove fruitful` 证明有成效　·　`a fruitful collaboration` 成果丰硕的合作
- **近义替换**　productive · rewarding · profitable
- **词族延伸**　fruit n. → fruitful adj. → fruitless adj. 无果的，徒劳的
- **易混辨析**　fruitful 强调「有实际成果」；productive 强调「产量高、产出多」，可指工厂也可指土地
- **加分句式**　Patience is rarely written into business plans, yet it is often the most fruitful investment. 耐心很少被写进商业计划书，却往往是最有回报的投资。

#### 19 · frustrate　`/frʌˈstreɪt/`　*vt.*

> 故事义 受挫　·　常考义 挫败；使灰心

- **核心搭配**　`frustrate the plan` 使计划受挫　·　`be frustrated by` 因…而沮丧　·　`frustrate an attempt` 挫败一次企图　·　`feel frustrated` 感到灰心
- **近义替换**　thwart · discourage · baffle
- **词族延伸**　frustration n. 沮丧；受挫 → frustrated adj. 灰心的 → frustrating adj. 令人沮丧的
- **易混辨析**　frustrate 指使「努力落空」或「人心灰意冷」；prevent 只指「阻止发生」，不含情绪
- **加分句式**　Every rule that cannot be enforced frustrates the ones who try to obey it. 每一条无法执行的规则，挫伤的恰恰是那些试图遵守它的人。

#### 20 · fulfill　`/fʊlˈfɪl/`　*vt.*

> 故事义 兑现　·　常考义 履行；实现；完成

- **核心搭配**　`fulfill a promise` 兑现承诺　·　`fulfill one's duty` 履行职责　·　`fulfill a contract` 履行合同　·　`fulfill the requirements` 满足要求
- **近义替换**　accomplish · carry out · meet
- **词族延伸**　fulfillment n. 履行；满足感 → fulfilling adj. 有成就感的
- **易混辨析**　fulfill 后接 promise / duty / potential 等抽象宾语；finish 只接具体任务（finish the work）
- **加分句式**　A company that cannot fulfill its promises to employees will not keep its customers for long. 连对员工的承诺都兑现不了的公司，留不住多久的客户。

#### 21 · function　`/ˈfʌŋkʃn/`　*n. / vi.*

> 故事义 职能　·　常考义 功能；作用；职责；运行；起作用

- **核心搭配**　`the function of …` 的功能　·　`fulfill a function` 履行职能　·　`function as` 起…的作用　·　`a social function` 社交宴会
- **近义替换**　role · purpose · work 运转
- **词族延伸**　functional adj. 功能的；实用的 → malfunction v./n. 故障
- **易混辨析**　function as 表「充当、起…作用」（The sofa functions as a bed）；function 作「宴会」是正式义项，阅读中常见
- **加分句式**　The real function of a headline is not to inform but to decide what deserves attention. 标题真正的职能不是提供信息，而是决定什么值得关注。

#### 22 · fund　`/fʌnd/`　*n. / v.*

> 故事义 提供资金　·　常考义 资金；基金；为…提供资金；资助

- **核心搭配**　`raise funds` 筹款　·　`a pension fund` 养老基金　·　`public funds` 公共资金　·　`fund a project` 资助一个项目
- **近义替换**　finance · capital · subsidize
- **词族延伸**　funding n. 拨款 → funded adj. 有资金的 → fund-raising n. 募捐
- **易混辨析**　fund 作动词是「出资、资助」；found 是「创立（组织）」，只差一个字母，形近易混
- **加分句式**　Whoever funds the research owns its questions, if not always its answers. 谁资助研究，谁就拥有它的问题——哪怕不一定拥有它的答案。

#### 23 · fundamental　`/ˌfʌndəˈmentl/`　*adj. / n.*

> 故事义 根本的　·　常考义 基础的；根本的；十分重大的

- **核心搭配**　`a fundamental change` 根本性的变化　·　`a fundamental right` 基本权利　·　`the fundamentals of …` 的基本原理　·　`be fundamental to` 对…至关重要
- **近义替换**　basic · essential · underlying
- **词族延伸**　fundament n. 基础 → fundamentally adv. 根本上
- **易混辨析**　fundamental 强调「根基性的、牵一发动全身」；basic 只指「基础的、初级的」，语气弱得多
- **加分句式**　The most fundamental question in economics is still who gets to decide what is produced. 经济学最根本的问题，仍然是：由谁来决定生产什么。

#### 24 · evidence　`/ˈevɪdəns/`　*n.*

> 故事义 证据　·　常考义 根据；证据

- **核心搭配**　`evidence of / for …` 的证据　·　`give evidence` 作证　·　`in evidence` 明显地；在作证　·　`a piece of evidence` 一条证据
- **近义替换**　proof · testimony · grounds
- **词族延伸**　evident adj. 明显的 → evidently adv.
- **易混辨析**　evidence 是不可数名词，不说 an evidence；proof 可数，可指抽象或具体的「证明」
- **加分句式**　Evidence does not speak for itself; someone must decide which of it gets heard. 证据不会自己说话；总得有人决定哪些证据可以被听见。

#### 25 · evident　`/ˈevɪdənt/`　*adj.*

> 故事义 明显的　·　常考义 明显的

- **核心搭配**　`it is evident that...` 显而易见…　·　`be evident from` 从…中可以看出　·　`become increasingly evident` 越来越明显
- **近义替换**　obvious · apparent · clear
- **词族延伸**　evidence n. → evident adj. → evidently adv.
- **易混辨析**　evident 是正式用词「明显可辨」；obvious 更口语化，且含「本应看出」的意味
- **加分句式**　It is evident from the data that the policy helped most those who needed it least. 数据清楚显示：这项政策最能惠及的人，恰恰是需求最小的那批。

#### 26 · evil　`/ˈiːvl/`　*adj. / n.*

> 故事义 邪恶　·　常考义 邪恶的；罪恶的；邪恶；罪恶

- **近义替换**　wicked · sinful · harmful
- **词族延伸**　evildoer n. 作恶者 → evil-minded adj. 心术不正的
- **易混辨析**　evil 指道德上的「恶」，程度深；bad 只是「不好」。necessary evil 是固定搭配，指「不得已而为之的坏事」
- **加分句式**　The most durable evils are rarely committed by villains, but by ordinary people following instructions. 最持久的恶行，很少出自反派之手，而是出自照章办事的普通人。

#### 27 · environment　`/ɪnˈvaɪrənmənt/`　*n.*

> 故事义 环境　·　常考义 环境；外界；自然环境

- **词族延伸**　environmental adj. 环境的 → environmentally adv. → environmentalist n. 环保主义者
- **易混辨析**　environment 兼指「自然环境」与「抽象环境」（工作环境、商业环境）；surroundings 只指具体的「周遭事物」
- **加分句式**　A competitive environment forces companies to improve; a protected one teaches them to complain. 竞争的环境逼企业进步；受保护的环境只会教它们抱怨。

#### 28 · enterprise　`/ˈentəpraɪz/`　*n.*

- **词族延伸**　enterprising adj. 有进取心的 → entrepreneur n. 企业家
- **易混辨析**　enterprise 兼指「企业」「事业」「进取心」；entrepreneur 只指「创业者、企业家」，注意词形区分
- **加分句式**　Every great enterprise begins as an arrangement that reasonable people advised against. 每一项伟大的事业，起初都是明理之人劝你别做的安排。

#### 29 · entertain　`/ˌentəˈteɪn/`　*v.*

> 故事义 娱乐　·　常考义 使…娱乐；使有兴趣

- **词族延伸**　entertainment n. 娱乐 → entertaining adj. 有趣的 → entertainer n. 艺人
- **易混辨析**　entertain 除「娱乐」外还有僻义「怀有、考虑」（entertain a doubt）；amuse 只指「逗乐」
- **加分句式**　A show that only entertains is forgotten by morning; one that also asks questions lingers for years. 只图娱乐的节目，天亮即被遗忘；顺带提问的节目，能让人记很多年。

#### 30 · entertainment　`/ˌentəˈteɪnmənt/`　*n.*

> 故事义 娱乐业　·　常考义 文娱节目

- **近义替换**　amusement · recreation · show
- **词族延伸**　entertain v. → entertainment n. → entertainer n.
- **易混辨析**　entertainment 是「娱乐这一活动或行业」；amusement 侧重「被逗乐的那一瞬间」
- **加分句式**　When entertainment becomes an industry, attention becomes its raw material and boredom its waste. 当娱乐成为产业，注意力就是它的原料，无聊就是它的废料。

#### 31 · monopoly　`/məˈnɒpəli/`　*n.*

> 故事义 垄断　·　常考义 垄断；专利（品）；垄断者（企业）

- **核心搭配**　`have a monopoly of / on` 垄断…　·　`break up a monopoly` 拆分垄断　·　`a state monopoly` 国家专营　·　`grant a monopoly` 授予专营权
- **近义替换**　domination · corner · cartel 卡特尔
- **词族延伸**　monopolize v. 垄断 → monopolist n. 垄断者 → monopolistic adj. 垄断的
- **易混辨析**　monopoly 是「市场垄断」；monotony 是「单调」，同词根 mono- 但词义领域完全不同
- **加分句式**　A monopoly on information is the only monopoly that never admits it is one. 对信息的垄断，是唯一从不承认自己是垄断的垄断。

#### 32 · monotonous　`/məˈnɒtənəs/`　*adj.*

> 故事义 单调的　·　常考义 单调无聊的；毫无变化的

- **近义替换**　tedious · dull · repetitive
- **词族延伸**　monotony n. 单调 → monotonously adv.
- **易混辨析**　monotonous 强调「无变化的重复」；tedious 强调「冗长得让人厌烦」，未必重复
- **加分句式**　Work that is monotonous is not always worthless; rivers carve canyons by repeating themselves. 单调的工作未必无价值——河流正是靠重复自己，才切出了峡谷。

## 03 · The Transplant｜移植

**题材** 科技 / 医疗　　**收录** 16 词：mood · transmission · transmit · transplant · transport · stock · store · storage · stride · strike · striking · string · structure · stun · primary · prime

### 英文故事

The **mood** in Ward 6 changed the day Dr. Luo announced the organ-sharing network. Until then, a successful **transplant** had depended on luck: a donor in one city, a recipient in another, a helicopter that might or might not **transport** the organ in time. The new system would **transmit** the donor's data to every matching hospital within seconds — a **transmission** that once took four phone calls and a prayer.

Building it was harder. Luo had to **store** donor records across three provinces, keep a cold **storage** chain unbroken, and **stock** every node with surgical teams on rotation. The **structure** of the network survived its first test by accident — a storm **struck** on launch night, knocking out power in two cities, and yet the organs arrived. 'Luck,' the engineers said. Luo said nothing and checked the logs.

Within a year the program took a great **stride** forward: waiting lists shortened by a fifth, and the results were so **striking** that sceptics were **stunned** into silence. The **primary** obstacle was no longer technology but money; the **prime** concern, Luo told the ministry, was to keep the network public. Behind him, a **string** of red dots lit up the map — one for every organ on its way to a stranger.

#### 33 · mood　`/muːd/`　*n.*

- **加分句式**　The mood of a workplace is set less by its walls than by what happens when someone makes a mistake. 一个工作场所的气氛，更多取决于有人犯错时会发生什么，而不是它的装修。

#### 35 · transmit　`/trænzˈmɪt/`　*v.*

- **易混辨析**　transmit 强调「从一点发送到另一点」，可指信号、数据或疾病；transfer 强调「位置或所有权的转移」
- **加分句式**　A network can transmit a message in seconds, but it cannot transmit the weight of a hand on a shoulder. 网络可以在几秒内传送一条消息，却传送不了搭在肩头那只手的分量。

#### 36 · transplant　`/trænsˈplɑːnt/`　*v. / n.*

- **易混辨析**　transplant 指「连根移走再种下」，可指器官、植物或人；transfer 只指「转移」，不含「重新扎根」
- **加分句式**　A successful transplant saves one life; a successful system saves the next hundred. 一次成功的移植救一个人，一套成功的系统救之后的一百个。

- **易混辨析**　transport 泛指「运送」；traffic 指「交通（流量）」，不指运输行为本身

- **加分句式**　The most striking feature of the reform was not what it changed but what it refused to change. 这场改革最显著之处，不是它改了什么，而是它拒绝改什么。

## 04 · The Verdict｜判决

**题材** 法律 / 社会　　**收录** 16 词：primitive · principal · principle · psychiatry · psychology · liability · liable · liberal · liberate · license · innocent · innovation · anguish · annoy · annual · anchor

### 英文故事

For eleven years the file of Ma Dezhong, an **innocent** man serving a life sentence, gathered dust in the appeals court. His mother mailed an **annual** letter to three newspapers, and a night-shift **anchor** at the local station kept reading it aloud. The family's **anguish** slowly turned into background noise — an open file that seemed to **annoy** no one in particular.

The turn came from an **innovation** no one had planned around: old interrogation tapes, re-examined. A professor of **psychology** testified that the confession had been extracted by methods **primitive** even for 2003, and a specialist in **psychiatry** described the cell where Ma had spent two years in solitary. The **principal** witness had recanted in 2019; the **principle** at stake, the defence argued, was older than any statute — the state must prove, not presume.

The court found the province **liable** and ordered compensation, though no sum could discharge a **liability** measured in eleven years. The laboratory that had handled the original samples lost its **license** that same month. The judge, a **liberal** appointed the previous spring, read the ruling without looking up; at four in the afternoon the gates opened, and by evening the headlines announced that Ma Dezhong had been **liberated**. The night-shift anchor read the verdict twice, slowly, in case anyone had missed it the first time.

<summary><b>03 · The Transplant｜移植</b></summary>

罗医生宣布器官共享网络的那一天，六病区的气氛变了。在此之前，一例成功的移植全靠运气：供体在一座城市，受者在另一座，直升机未必能把器官及时运达。新系统会在几秒钟内把供体数据传送给每一家配型医院——而这样的信息传递，过去需要四通电话加一次祈祷。

搭建它要难得多。罗必须在三个省份储存供体档案，保持低温储藏链不断链，还要给每个节点配备轮班的手术团队。网络体系第一次经受考验纯属意外——上线当夜一场风暴来袭，两座城市停电，器官却照样送达。工程师们说：「运气。」罗什么也没说，去查了日志。

一年之内，这个项目大踏步前进：等候名单缩短了五分之一，成果之显著，让怀疑者震惊得哑口无言。首要障碍不再是技术而是钱；罗对部里说，最要紧的问题是保住网络的公共属性。他身后的屏幕上，一连串红点照亮了地图——每一个红点，都是一颗正在送往陌生人那里的器官。

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>04 · The Verdict｜判决</b></summary>

十一年来，马德忠的案卷在上诉法院积灰——这个无辜的人正在服无期徒刑。他的母亲每年给三家报纸寄一封信，当地电视台一名夜班主持人总把它念出来。这家人的极度痛苦渐渐成了背景音——一份敞开的卷宗，似乎并不特别惹恼任何人。

转机来自一项没人料到的革新：重新审查当年的审讯录音。一位心理学教授作证说，那份口供是用即便放到2003年也属原始的方法逼出来的；一位精神病学专家描述了马德忠独居两年的牢房。主要证人早在2019年就翻供了；辩方说，此案牵涉的原则比任何法条都古老——国家必须证明，而不能推定。

法院裁定省里负有法律责任并判令赔偿，尽管没有一笔钱能清偿以十一年计的责任。那个最初经办样本的实验室同月失去了执照。那位前一年春天上任的开明法官宣读裁决时头也不抬；下午四点，大门打开，到傍晚，头条已宣布马德忠获释。夜班主持人把判决书念了两遍，很慢，生怕有人第一遍没听清。

</details>
